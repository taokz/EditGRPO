## What cannot be reproduced without the heavy stack
- Throughput: single-process rollouts for 3B with 4096-token reports are slow without vLLM; `rollout.py` exposes a `Sampler` interface so nano-vllm can be dropped in.
- Clinical rewards and the RaTE extractor need `radgraph`, `f1chexbert`, `RaTEScore` (+ transformers) and GPU; they are lazy plugins. Parity with the Flask server must be checked on GPU (STATUS.md).
- Real-weight equivalence of `model.py` vs HF Qwen2.5-VL is a GPU follow-up; CPU tests use a tiny random config.
